The CLARIPHY AI Collaboration Meeting (Pyle Center, UW-Madison, September
14-16, 2026) brought together collider, neutrino, nuclear, and astroparticle
physics researchers to establish a national-scale AI collaboration for
experimental particle physics, building on
[the community-vision whitepaper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17582) in this
list. It organized working groups around Data and Benchmarks, Foundation Models,
Agentic Workflows, Computing Infrastructure, and Education and Workforce
Development. Its Analysis Agentic Workflows breakout session reviewed exactly
the scattered tooling this repository now indexes — JFC, the IRIS-HEP and
USATLAS marketplaces, HTCondor MCP, Rucio MCP — and is this repository's direct
origin.

Useful when: you want the context for why this repository exists, or pointers to
the other CLARIPHY working groups (foundation models, benchmarks,
infrastructure).

Not useful when: you're looking for a tool rather than background — see the
other categories in this list instead.
