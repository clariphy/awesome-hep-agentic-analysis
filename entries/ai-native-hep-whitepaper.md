A community whitepaper, "Building an AI-native Research Ecosystem for
Experimental Particle Physics: A Community Vision," describing how AI can
accelerate discovery in experimental particle physics — grand challenges, how
current and planned facilities (HL-LHC, DUNE, EIC) can implement the vision, and
what a fully AI-native future experiment (FCC-ee, IceCube-Gen2, a muon collider)
might look like. It calls for national-scale collaboration across DOE
laboratories and universities, and is the document that directly motivated the
CLARIPHY AI Collaboration Meeting (see
[the workshop entry](https://indico.global/event/18639/) in this list), whose
Analysis Agentic Workflows session in turn motivated this repository.

Useful when: you want the community-level case and roadmap for AI-native HEP
research, or context for why efforts like this list, JFC, and the marketplaces
exist.

Not useful when: you're looking for a specific tool to run — this is a vision
document, not software.
